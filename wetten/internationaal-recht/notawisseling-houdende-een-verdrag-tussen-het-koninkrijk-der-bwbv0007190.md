# Notawisseling houdende een verdrag tussen het Koninkrijk der Nederlanden en de Republiek Kosovo inzake de privileges en immuniteiten van verbindingsofficieren die door Kosovo bij Europol gedetacheerd worden

MINISTRY OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS

The Hague,

11 November 2026

Treaties Division

Reference no. BZ2633688

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Kingdom of the Netherlands presents its compliments to the Embassy of the Republic of Kosovo, and has the honour to propose, with reference to the Working Arrangement establishing cooperative relations between the law enforcement authorities of Kosovo and the European Police Office of 28 July 2020 (hereinafter 'the Working Arrangement'), and in accordance with the provisions of Article 8(5) and Article 63(2) of Regulation (EU) 2016/794 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 May 2016 on the European Union Agency for Law Enforcement Cooperation (Europol), which state that Europol liaison officers shall enjoy the privileges and immunities necessary for the performance of their tasks, be agreed upon as set out in the [Attachment](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBV0007190) to this Note.

If this proposal is acceptable to the Embassy of the Republic of Kosovo, the Ministry proposes that this Note and the affirmative Note of the Embassy shall constitute an Agreement between the Kingdom of the Netherlands and the Republic of Kosovo, which shall be applied provisionally from the date of the affirmative note and which shall enter into force on the first day of the second month following the date on which the Kingdom of the Netherlands and the Republic of Kosovo have informed each other that the formalities required for the entry into force have been complied with.

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Kingdom of the Netherlands avails itself of this opportunity to renew to the Embassy of the Republic of Kosovo the assurances of its highest consideration.

Embassy of the Republic of Kosovo

The Hague
