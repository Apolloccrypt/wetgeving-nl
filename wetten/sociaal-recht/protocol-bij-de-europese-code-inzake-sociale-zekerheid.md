# Protocol bij de Europese Code inzake Sociale Zekerheid

*Preamble The member States of the Council of Europe, signatory hereto, Being resolved to establish a higher level of social security than that provided for in the provisions of the European Code of Social Security signed at Strasbourg on the 16th April 1964 (hereinafter referred to as “the Code”); Desirous that all member States of the Council should strive to achieve such higher level, with due regard to economic considerations in their respective countries, Have agreed on the following provisions which have been prepared with the collaboration of the International Labour Office:*

#### SECTION I

*Wijzigt de Europese Code inzake sociale zekerheid; Straatsburg, 16 april 1964.*

#### SECTION II

##### Artikel 1

No member State of the Council of Europe shall sign or ratifiy this Protocol without having simultaneously or previously signed or ratified the [European Code of Social Security](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465).

##### Artikel 2

No State shall accede to this Protocol without having simultaneously or previously acceded to the [European Code of Social Security](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465).

#### SECTION III

##### Artikel 1

This Protocol shall be open to signature by the member States. It shall be subject to ratification. Instruments of ratification shall be deposited with the Secretary-General, provided that the Committee of Ministers in appropriate cases has previously given an affirmative decision as provided for in Section IV, paragraph 4.

##### Artikel 2

This Protocol shall enter into force one year after the date of the deposit of the third instrument of ratification.

##### Artikel 3

As regards any Signatory ratifying subsequently, this Protocol shall enter into force one year after the date of deposit of its instrument of ratification.

#### SECTION IV

##### Artikel 1

Any Signatory wishing to avail itself of the provisions of [Article 2, paragraph 2 of the Code](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465&artikel=2) as amended by the Protocol shall, before ratification, submit to the Secretary-General a report showing to what extent its system of social security is in conformity with the provisions of this Protocol.

Such report shall include a statement of:

- (a) the relevant laws and regulations; and

- (b) evidence of compliance with the statistical conditions specified in the following provisions of the Code as amended by this Protocol:

   - (i) [Articles 9 (a), (b) or (c)](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465&artikel=9); [15 (a) or (b)](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465&artikel=15); [21 (a)](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465&artikel=21); [27 (a) or (b)](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465&artikel=27); [33](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465&artikel=33); [41 (a) or (b)](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465&artikel=41); [48 (a) or (b)](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465&artikel=48); [55 (a) or b)](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465&artikel=55); [61 (a) or (b)](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465&artikel=61), as regards the number of persons protected;

   - (ii) [Articles 44](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465&artikel=44), [65](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465&artikel=65), [66](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465&artikel=66) or [67](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465&artikel=67), as regards the rates of benefit;

   - (iii) [Article 24, paragraph 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465&artikel=24), as regards duration of unemployment benefit; and

   - (iv) [Article 70, paragraph 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465&artikel=70), as regards the proportion of the financial resources constituted by the insurance contributions of employees protected; and

- (c) all elements which the Signatory wishes to be taken into acount, in accordance with [Article 2, paragraphs 2 and 3 of the Code](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465&artikel=2) as amended by this Protocol.

Such evidence shall, as far as possible, be presented in such general order and manner as may be suggested by the Committee.

##### Artikel 2

The Signatory shall furnish to the Secretary-General, if so requested by him, further information on the manner in which its system of social security is in conformity with the provisions of this Protocol.

##### Artikel 3

Such report and further information shall be examined by the Committee which shall take into account the provisions of [Article 2, paragraph 3 of the Code](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0004465&artikel=2). The Committee shall submit to the Committee of Ministers a report containing its conclusions.

##### Artikel 4

The Committee of Ministers shall, by a two-thirds majority in accordance with [Article 20, paragraph (d) of the Statute of the Council of Europe](https://wetten.overheid.nl/jci1.3:c:BWBV0005506&artikel=20), decide whether the system of social security of such Signatory is in conformity with the requirements of this Protocol.

##### Artikel 5

If the Committee of Ministers decides that the said social security scheme is not in conformity with the provisions of this Protocol, it shall so inform the Signatory concerned and may make recommendations as to how such conformity may be effected.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the undersigned, being duly authorised thereto, have signed this Protocol.

DONE at Strasbourg, this 16th day of April 1964 in French and English, both texts being equally authoritative, in a single copy which shall remain deposited in the archives of the Council of Europe, and of which the Secretary-General shall send certified copies to each of the signatory and acceding States and to the Director-General of the International Labour Office.
